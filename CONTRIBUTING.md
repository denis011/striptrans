# Doprinosi / Contributing

Hvala na interesovanju! Za sada **ne primam izmene koda (pull request-ove)**: autor želi da zadrži slobodu da
kasnije menja licencu projekta. Greške i predloge prijavi kao *issue* — to mnogo pomaže.

Thank you for your interest! For now **pull requests are not accepted**, so that the author keeps the freedom to
change the project's license later. Please report bugs and ideas as issues — that helps a lot.
